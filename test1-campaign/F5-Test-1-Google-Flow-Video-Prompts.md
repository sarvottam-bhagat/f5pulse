### Format

- 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 30 fps.
- Video 1 is 32 seconds: four 8-second Flow clips. Video 2 is 36 seconds: four 8-second clips plus one 4-second CTA clip.
- Important text inside the center 80% safe area so it remains readable on LinkedIn and Instagram.
- No shot should feel static for longer than 1.5 seconds. Motion can be micro-motion: a 2-4% push, a masking line, a counting ring, a drifting paper layer, or a selection-box draw-on.

### Brand palette

- Near-black: `#070A09`
- Warm bone: `#F3F0E8`
- Soft gallery grey: `#D9D9D3`
- F5 green accent: `#23D66F`
- Dark green: `#0B5D35`
- Use the blue, red, yellow, and green corners of the official F5 logo only as tiny navigation or progress accents.
- Replace the purple neon of the references with F5 green. Avoid rainbow gradients.

### Typography

- Headline: Inter or Manrope ExtraBold, tight tracking, mixed case.
- Supporting line: Inter Medium.
- Editorial/typewriter moments: Cormorant Garamond or Libre Baskerville.
- Numbers: Inter Black with tabular figures.
- Captions: Inter SemiBold, white text in a translucent black rounded rectangle; active spoken phrase highlighted in F5 green.
- Exact text is added in Google Vids, not baked into the generated visual plates.

### Motion language

- Clean editorial composition with large negative space.
- Masks and panels move on straight horizontal or vertical axes.
- Primary entrances use a fast 120-180ms blur/smear followed by a 250-350ms ease-out settle.
- Cards and portraits may enter at 108-112% scale and settle to 100% with one small overshoot. Never use repeated bouncy social-media presets.
- Thin dotted circles, barcode lines, measurement ticks, guide grids, and selection handles suggest a design workspace.
- Transitions alternate between:
  1. neon line or search-field morph;
  2. horizontal RGB/glitch smear lasting no more than 6 frames;
  3. paper or black-bar wipe;
  4. thin-outline box wipe with cloud/fog reveal.
- Camera behavior: mostly locked and graphic; restrained 2-5% push-ins; one short 3D parallax move per video; no sweeping drone shots or chaotic handheld movement.

### Finishing

- Subtle film grain at 3-5% opacity.
- Soft shadow under paper/sculpture cutouts.
- Highlights gently bloom around green neon, but text stays sharp.
- Warm-neutral midtones, deep true blacks, clean whites.
- No generic corporate stock footage, blue office gradients, lens-flare overload, or glossy tech tunnels.

# Global audio and finishing instructions

## Voiceover

- Generate one continuous voiceover per video in Google Vids, not separate voices for each clip.
- Keep the same voice across the full sequence.
- Speech must begin within the first 0.25 seconds.
- Remove breaths or pauses longer than 250ms.
- Aim for conversational pacing and emphasize only the proof phrases.

## Music

- Music should sit about 18-22 dB below the voice during narration.
- Use automatic balancing/ducking in Google Vids with the voiceover marked as narrative and music marked as background.
- Allow a 400ms music swell between spoken clauses, then return under the voice.
- Fade in over 250ms and resolve cleanly during the final 500ms.

## Sound effects
