# F5 Test 1 — Three Posts

## Content strategy

The two brands speak to opposite sides of the same employment relationship:

- **F5 Hiring Solutions** speaks to U.S. business owners and hiring leaders who need dependable full-time capacity without building an overseas operation themselves.
- **F5 Global Talent** speaks to professionals in India and the Philippines who want structured, long-term remote work with U.S. companies and local employment support.

The mix below gives F5 Hiring Solutions two demand-generation posts and F5 Global Talent one candidate-value post. It deliberately avoids three near-identical service claims.

## Post 2 — A better shortlist starts with a better brief

**Brand:** F5 Hiring Solutions  
**Audience:** U.S. founders, COOs, department heads, and hiring managers  
**Format:** 1080 × 1350 LinkedIn/Instagram static  
**Visual:** `post-02-better-hiring-brief.png`

### Publish-ready copy

A better shortlist starts before the first résumé.

A weak brief says: “We need a project manager.”

A useful one answers four questions:

1. What problem must this person own?
2. Which skills are genuinely non-negotiable?
3. When do they need to overlap with your team?
4. What should success look like after 90 days?

That context is what separates a pile of profiles from a shortlist you can actually interview.

At F5, discovery comes first. Then we screen and shortlist for the work—not just the title.

If you are hiring next quarter, define the outcome now.

## Post 3 — Ready for a U.S.-hours role?

**Brand:** F5 Global Talent  
**Audience:** Skilled professionals in India and the Philippines considering long-term remote work with U.S. teams  
**Format:** 1080 × 1350 LinkedIn/Instagram static  
**Visual:** `post-03-us-hours-readiness.png`

### Publish-ready copy

Before you apply for a U.S.-hours remote role, ask yourself four honest questions:

• Can I protect a consistent schedule?  
• Can I explain my work clearly to a distributed team?  
• Do I enjoy owning outcomes without someone beside me?  
• Am I looking for a long-term, full-time role—not a temporary side project?

There is no right answer for everyone. But clarity now prevents frustration later.

F5 Global Talent hires professionals in India and the Philippines for full-time remote roles with U.S. companies, with F5 handling employment, equipment, payroll, HR, and ongoing support.

If that matches the way you want to work, explore current openings at f5globaltalent.com/jobs.

- All visual text is typeset rather than generated, preventing misspellings and distorted logos.

- Every post uses a distinct content job: sector relevance, hiring education, and candidate fit.
- Captions are written separately for the buyer and candidate audiences; the brands are not treated as interchangeable.
